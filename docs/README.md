# Blotter documentation

Run one client's next month through Blotter, then build up your firm's workflow from there.

Blotter keeps recurring client jobs, document requests, reviewer decisions and time for your own billing together. It is free, open source and MIT licensed. Your installation runs in your own account.

## Start here

- [Getting started](/docs/getting-started): run Blotter locally, finish setup and add your people.
- [Jobs and templates](/docs/jobs): start a job and roll it into the next period.
- [Client documents and review](/docs/documents-and-review): request files, accept them and record sign-off.
- [Time and billing](/docs/time-and-billing): record time and approve a fixed CSV export.
- [Move in](/docs/move-in): preview and import your client list.
- [Backup and restore](/docs/backup-and-restore): keep a practice archive and restore into an empty installation.
- [Agent access](/docs/agent-access): give an agent a key to read context and prepare work for a person to approve.

## What Blotter covers

Blotter is practice management for a small bookkeeping and accounting firm. Today it runs recurring client jobs, document requests, review and sign-off, capacity and time for your own billing. Due dates are entered by people, with their source. New amounts are recorded in US dollars; records from the first release keep their Australian dollars. Blotter does not file returns, calculate tax, keep client books or give advice. Billing exports are for your own billing system; Blotter does not send invoices or take payments yet.

The principal keeps the firm's US settings (address, EIN, entity, fiscal year end, default currency, time zone and staff notification hours) under **Team & safety**. Calculated federal and state deadlines, signatures, invoices and payments are the product's contract, and the [contract page](/contract) shows what is done and what is next. The [first-release acceptance scope](https://github.com/just-every/run-blotter/blob/main/ACCEPTANCE.md) records the journeys the code passes today. The [source repository](https://github.com/just-every/run-blotter) includes operational and development instructions.
