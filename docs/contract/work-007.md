# WORK-007 capability rules

The following source rules are retained from the contract at `ca2596bae4c081a2df169d327b23c0030428b918`. Current acceptance and company-policy classes are defined by CONTRACT.md.

**Capacity (CAPACITY-001, inherited before this ships):** the capacity view's service filter selects jobs by work type. 

**Semantics (WORK-007):** until an admin role exists the principal defines work types (name unique ignoring case, optional default template, default budget in minutes applied when a job is started without one, #rrggbb color) and sub-statuses (one stage each, unique by name within it, limited to selected work types or open to every type). Saving a template also creates a work type of the template's name with it as default template; a job carries the work type chosen when it is started, otherwise its template's, and a rolled-forward job keeps its predecessor's. A new job starts Planned; jobs that existed before the status model start In progress, with history beginning at their current stage as of their submission, sign-off or creation. Planned, Ready to start, In progress and Waiting (and their sub-statuses) are set by the job's people while it is open; In review is reached only by submission (or a client's approval that submits, recorded as that approval) and Completed only by sign-off; a return goes to In progress; entering In review or Completed clears the sub-status, and the database refuses any sub-status outside the job's current stage. Each history entry records the old and new stage and sub-status, who and when (UTC); a rename shows the new name throughout history; a deleted sub-status keeps its name in history. Deleting a sub-status that jobs use requires a preview naming the jobs and their target stage (open stages only for an open-stage sub-status, the same stage otherwise) and commits only with that preview's fingerprint, moving the jobs and recording each move. The queue filters by work type, stage and sub-status over the jobs a person may read; time in status runs from each entry to the next (or now), in whole minutes rounded down, with entries, jobs now in it, total and average. Each person keeps private saved views of the queue (work type, stage, sub-status by name). A sub-status may carry a client label: the client link shows it while the job is in that sub-status, otherwise the stage's name (PORTAL-003). 

**Job roles (WORK-007):** a work type may also need a Manager and a Partner; a job of that type names them when it starts (jobs from recurring rules and bulk scheduling use the template's own type, which needs neither). The partner is an active reviewer or the principal and never the preparer (enforced on every reassignment, offboarding and handover); the reviewer's sign-off records the review and leaves the job In review – partner sign-off, and only the named partner or the principal completes or returns it; a return clears the recorded review. 

**Inherited before they ship:** AUTOMATE-001 status triggers and set-status actions, WORK-006 plans and boards, REPORT-001 status reports and the MIGRATE-001 Karbon import carry WORK-007's status model in their own text and acceptance; WORK-007 is done for every capability that exists today.
