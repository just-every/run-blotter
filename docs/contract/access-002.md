# ACCESS-002 capability rules

The following source rules are retained from the contract at `ca2596bae4c081a2df169d327b23c0030428b918`. Current acceptance and company-policy classes are defined by CONTRACT.md.

**Coverage (CAPACITY-002, inherited before this ships):** coverage proposals and named cover only use colleagues who hold the client's grant.

**Implemented native policy (sx-blotter-c36/c37, 22 Oct; approved with change by ct, batch 11 H):** The principal configures overlapping teams and explicit direct client grants.

**Ruled (ct 22 Oct):** the principal may grant named members two published permissions: 'manage access' and 'financial authority'. Each grant is audited and needs step-up. A holder can never grant itself, widen its own client scope or revoke the principal. Firm-wide financial reports open to financial-authority holders for the clients they hold. Export, sensitive-administration and credential-reveal step-up applies from installation, whether or not scopes are configured. 

**Semantic implemented (sx-blotter-c37):** the principal grants, replaces or revokes a member's permissions at `/access/delegations` (a reason is recorded; each change is audited and bumps the access revision, so the holder's concurrent writes fail and the next request already lacks the permission). A manage-access holder may change teams, individual grants and restricted documents only for clients it holds itself and only for teams and people whose existing scope lies inside its own holdings; it may not name itself, be a member of a team it changes, name the principal, or delegate. A financial-authority holder prepares, issues, sends and voids invoices (BILL-002), and reads the firm financial report, billing lists and exports for held clients only; the principal alone selects the invoice authority and confirms retention expiry. Delegations are portable with archives; step-ups and bearer shares are not. Client erasure removes staff/team client grants and document grants, redacts restriction reasons, retains attribution and fences older restores; archives retain grants but exclude step-ups and bearer shares. ACCESS-003/005 and API-002 inherit the same live scopes and sensitive step-up before new identities or integration authority ship.
