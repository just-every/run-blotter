# CRM-004 capability rules

The following source rules are retained from the contract at `ca2596bae4c081a2df169d327b23c0030428b918`. Current acceptance and company-policy classes are defined by CONTRACT.md.

An unchanged merge can be undone for 30 days.

**Matching (implemented subset):** proposals are worked out from the clients as they are when read, whatever created them; a pair is proposed when it shares a contact email (case-insensitive), a non-empty EIN (the client's tax id), or a name that is equal, or similar (at least two words each and at least 75% of their distinct words shared), once lower-cased and with accents, punctuation, "&"/"and" and business suffixes set aside.

**Authority (ct 19 Oct 03:36Z):** a merge of two clients with different non-empty EINs, or different non-empty contact emails, needs the principal's recorded authority reason (same entity; the surviving contact is authorized for both); reviewers merge only records whose EINs and contact emails agree or are blank; CRM-001 contacts and PORTAL-002 portal users add a per-contact check before they ship. Undo is available for 30 days while neither record has changed since the merge. 

**Merge and undo (semantics):** the person picks which client keeps its id (the survivor) and, for every field where the two differ (name, EIN, contact name and email, tags and each firm field), which value survives; each client keeps its own "came from" source. The other client stays as a read-only record pointing at the survivor and leaves every list and picker. Its jobs (with their tasks, time, documents, organizer answers, deliverables and upload links), discussions, tax notices, expenses, recurring rules, rate cards and group memberships move to the survivor, including signed-off jobs, resolved discussions, closed notices and decided or reimbursed expenses, whose only permitted change is that move, between the two clients of a merge that is not undone. A merge is refused while both clients have a job from the same template for the same period, or a recurring rule for the same template. Moved rate cards join the survivor's under TIME-003 precedence. Each import source row keeps the client it created; a re-import of that row still matches it, shows the survivor it now lives on and creates no client. Undo restores the recorded records, memberships and the survivor's own values exactly; it is open until the merge time plus 30 × 24 hours while the survivor has not been edited, merged again, or gained or lost a linked record since, and changes made inside moved records travel back with them. A merge made on the principal's authority is undone only by the principal. 

**Inherited:** each later capability moves with a merge and keeps its source IDs attached before it ships: CRM-001 contacts (including phone and SSN-digit matches), MAIL-001 email threads, BILL-002 invoices, PAY-001 payments, PORTAL-002 portal users, PORTAL-004 organizers, SIGN-001/SIGN-002 signatures, and the source IDs of MIGRATE-001–005 (Karbon, TaxDome, Canopy, Financial Cents, Double) and LEDGER-001/INTEGRATE-001 (QBO, Xero; exactly one ledger mapping per survivor).
