**Implemented native policy (sx-blotter-c37/c38, 22 Oct):** the principal or a reviewer records a client's Form 2848, Form 8821 or state authorization (representative, optional staff representative and CAF number, and one to ten tax matters with form and first/last year) as a draft; files its signed copy by linking a completed SIGN-002 request (a 2848 or 8821 only through a request that carried the remote-authentication attestation); records the submission (an electronically signed 2848 or 8821 only as IRS online submission, per IRS.gov/Submit2848); and records the agency's answer (recorded with the CAF number, or rejected with its reason) or a withdrawal.

**Form preparation (sx-blotter-c38):** staff review the recorded scope, signer authority, representative eligibility and replacement of prior authorizations, then prepare the official January 2021 IRS Form 2848 or 8821 from taxpayer and representative details. Values are flattened before SIGN-002 places the taxpayer signature/date and, for a 2848, the named staff representative's declaration signature/date. More than three matters use a line-3 continuation page. The TIN is kept only in that document, not audit detail or a separate client field. Preparation creates a reviewable signature draft and sends nothing. Staff supply a state agency's own equivalent through SIGN-002. A completed IRS request must match the authorization's form kind before it can be filed. Agency-contact entries name their tax form, and every year in the notice period must be covered; transcript and agency-contact writes recheck the recorded authorization inside the write. A continuing recurrence without an open job alerts the principal. INTEGRATE-006 inherits actual transcript retrieval and its recorded-authorization scope; DUE-002 and NOTICE-001 inherit this scope for future agency-contact doors. IRS submission and CAF acceptance here are practitioner-recorded facts, never automatic transmissions or inferred acceptance.
