**Semantics (CAPACITY-002):** a manager is a reviewer or the principal (the people who reschedule under WORK-004); they plan cover for a person on leave or over capacity between two dates (at most 92 days apart).

**Client authority (ACCESS-002, inherited before it ships):** until client grants exist every active member may work any client; when ACCESS-002 adds client and team grants, a proposed or named colleague must hold the client's grant. CAPACITY-002 is done for every capability that exists today.

**CAPACITY-002 — Reassignment and dependency aware scheduling.** Reassign and reschedule work when staff are absent or overloaded, proposing eligible coverage with client authority, skills, remaining capacity, dependency order and independent reviewer constraints.
