# COLLAB-001 capability rules


**COLLAB-001 — Contextual discussions and review alerts.** Staff discuss a client, task, document or review in internal threads with mentions, ownership, unread state and retained decisions.

**COLLAB-001 — Contextual discussions and review alerts.** Status: **done**. Submit work, receive the assigned reviewer's notification, mention a colleague and request a correction. Resolve it as the preparer, inspect the retained thread and verify that the client and unassigned staff cannot read private discussion.
