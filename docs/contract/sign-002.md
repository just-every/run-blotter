The following source rules are retained from the contract at `ca2596bae4c081a2df169d327b23c0030428b918`. Current acceptance and company-policy classes are defined by CONTRACT.md.

**Offboarding (ACCESS-006, inherited before this ships):** pending signature requests sent in a departing staff member's name appear in the offboarding preview and move to the work successor in its approved batch; completed signatures keep their original requester.

**Inherited MSG-002 marketing consent (ct 20 Oct 21:21Z):** Marketing consent is never bundled into a signature request, nor made a condition of service. 

**Receiving ERASURE-002 (ct 22 Oct 2026):** Before shipping, SIGN-002 adds its records and provider copies to ERASURE-002's classes, clocks, holds, preview, erasure, receipt and restore fence (ct 22 Oct 2026). 

**Native semantics (sx-blotter-c36/c37, 22 Oct; approved with change by ct, batch 11 H):** Staff with full job scope freeze the original valid unencrypted PDF, signer relationships, order and signature/initial/date/text field geometry, then explicitly send through existing entity/contact-bound portal links. Every contact signer must be active, authorized for the entity, a signer, portal-enabled and consented for requests/email; a staff representative signer is an active member or the principal who holds the client with working access. The portal exposes only that signer's fields and requires explicit document-specific ESIGN/UETA consent and intent, never marketing consent. Sequential signatures wait for every prior signer; parallel signatures can arrive in either order, with revision guards preventing lost updates. Decline records a reason; resending a declined or restored request starts a fresh attempt and preserves prior attempts. Voiding prevents further signatures. Completion flattens entered fields onto the original PDF and creates a separate downloadable certificate with order, name, contact/link id, consent wording/version, times and document hashes. It truthfully states typed electronic signatures and portal-possession authentication, not verified identity, KBA or a cryptographic digital signature. 

**Ruled (ct 22 Oct):** at consent and at signing, Blotter records the connection IP and user agent. Both appear on the certificate and are retained and erased with the signed record. For a document marked Form 2848 or 8821, a signer's signature is accepted only after a staff member records a remote-authentication attestation: the method (photo ID by selfie or video plus a secondary document, or personal knowledge), the verifier, the date and the items checked. The certificate states the form is electronically signed and may be submitted only online at IRS.gov/Submit2848. Staff representatives can be signers through their own session. A PDF whose text names Form 8879, 8879-S/PE/C/F or 8878 is refused for SIGN-001. 

**Semantic implemented (sx-blotter-c37):** consent and signature are one explicit submission, so the connection IP (the edge's connecting address) and user agent are captured once at that moment for each signer, stored only on the signer record, printed on the certificate as the consent-and-signature connection, and redacted with the signer record when the signed record is erased; they never enter the audit log. A document is a Form 2848 or 8821 when its form kind, title or the PDF's own text names it; the attestation is recorded per remote (contact) signer and signing attempt by a staff member with the job, with method photo ID by selfie or video (name, SSN or ITIN, address and date of birth checked against a named secondary document) or personal knowledge (with how it was established), the verifier's name and a date not in the future; it cannot be changed once recorded, and a resend needs a fresh attestation. A staff representative is named as a signer, receives no portal link, and signs with the same consent and intent at `/signatures/:id/staff-sign` in their own authenticated session (the session id stands in for the portal link on the certificate); they need no attestation. The 8879/8878 check reads the PDF text as well as the form kind, title and file name. A deactivated staff signer can no longer sign; the request is voided and re-requested. AUTHZ-001 inherits this attestation and the Submit2848 statement when it prepares 2848/8821 records. Staff/client work views file the signed PDF, certificate and timeline on the source client/work. Pending requests move atomically during offboarding; completed requester attribution stays. Signature-complete events use existing notification preferences, quiet hours, live authority and deduplication, and the automation source is rechecked inside its once-only effect batch. Signed documents join ERASURE-002 signed_documents clocks from completion or later job sign-off, class/job/record holds, preview, expiry, counts, receipt and stale-restore fence; unfinished requests block erasure until completed or voided. Restored pending/declined requests stay held and old bearer links remain excluded. Outside mail/download copies name their holders. Erased-client signed records disappear from normal lists and use opaque expiry labels; explicit retained detail/PDF doors require the principal and attribute the read. SIGN-001 retains its separate required KBA and IP/login/KBA-result evidence; general signing refuses IRS e-file authorization forms (by label or PDF text) or any form marked KBA-required until that provider path ships. FOLLOWUP-001 inherits unsigned-request chasing before its schedule door ships.

**SIGN-002 — General document signature requests.** Staff request signatures on any firm document, for example a Form 2848 or 8821 (AUTHZ-001), a bank authorization, a management representation letter or a W-9.

**SIGN-002 — General document signature requests.** Status: **done**. Place fields on a PDF and request sequential signatures from two contacts. The first signer declines with a reason; resend the request, then complete both signatures. Inspect the signed file, its certificate, the signing order and the timeline on the client and work item. A voided request cannot be signed afterwards, and a contact who is not a signer cannot open it. Offboard a preparer with a pending signature request (ACCESS-006): it is listed in the preview, follows the successor, and none is left owned by the deactivated member. Before activation, satisfy BASE-001’s inherited authority/audit/erasure/portability gate with named public-door evidence.
