# DUE-002 capability rules


**DUE-002 — Tax notices and resolution work.** Capture a federal/state notice with its source file, agency, tax period, response date, assigned practitioner, authorization and resolution plan.

**DUE-002 — Tax notices and resolution work.** Status: **done**. Open a notice from a client upload, verify the response deadline, assign a practitioner, link an authorized transcript and response receipt, and schedule follow-up. A failed response stays open; closing requires evidence and a recorded outcome.
