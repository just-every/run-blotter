# TIME-004 capability rules


**TIME-004 — Expenses and disbursements.** Record billable and non-billable expenses (filing fees, postage, mileage at the firm-set rate, software pass-throughs) against a client and work item.

**TIME-004 — Expenses and disbursements.** Status: **done**. Record a state filing fee with a receipt and a 10% markup, approve it, invoice it and inspect the invoice line and WIP. A non-billable expense stays off the invoice and appears in profitability. An unapproved expense cannot be invoiced.
