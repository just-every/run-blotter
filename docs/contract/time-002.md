# TIME-002 capability rules


**TIME-002 — Timesheet approval and period locks.** Staff submit timesheets to managers, who approve or return entries with reasons.

**TIME-002 — Timesheet approval and period locks.** Status: **done**. Submit a weekly timesheet, return and correct one entry, approve it and close the period. Attempt direct edits and duplicate billing, then post an authorized adjustment and inspect its history.
