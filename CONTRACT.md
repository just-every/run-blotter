# Blotter product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Blotter is the practice manager a US bookkeeping or accounting firm uses to complete client engagements from signed scope through recurring work, communications, document collection, review and payment. It replaces Karbon, TaxDome or Canopy and offers the connected close and small-firm automation depth buyers seek from Financial Cents and Double. USD and US practice norms are the default. Difficulty building provider applications, bank/payment connections, desktop bridges or native apps is work to deliver, not a smaller product boundary.

This is the product destination. Status below describes current code, not a reduction of that destination. The previous first-release ACCEPTANCE.md described an Australian BAS-style workflow with manual deadlines, link uploads, independent review and billing CSVs; there was no root CONTRACT.md or existing product clause ID to rename. Its useful guarantees are retained here. Every clause is therefore classified as target or push-further, including implemented foundations; Published has zero clauses. Existing UI, homepage and runtime behavior are unchanged by this document.

## How acceptance is judged

Every named clause gives buyer behavior and an acceptance journey through the app, authenticated HTTP API, CLI or authorized provider door. ACCEPTANCE.md lists the same journeys and statuses. A cited local file/test proves only its stated implemented subset; it does not prove real-provider acceptance, tax authority access, native distribution, production capacity or accessibility. Real-provider journeys use authorized accounts; automated tests fake only paid/network edges. Source freshness, provider acknowledgement, settlement and human judgment are distinct facts.

## US practice and client engagements

- **US-001 — US firm setup and currency.** A US principal configures a firm in USD with US addresses, EIN/entity classifications, fiscal years, service lines, time zones and notification hours. Money uses integer cents and explicit currency; US 1099/W-2, federal/state tax and sales-tax engagements have appropriate terminology. Historical AUD records retain their currency and are never relabelled USD. **Acceptance evidence:** Set up a US bookkeeping and tax practice, add an LLC and individual client, select USD and America/New_York, and create payroll, sales-tax and annual-tax work. Inspect currency and dates across a daylight-saving change; imported AUD history remains AUD.

- **CRM-001 — Client entities and contacts.** Manage individuals, businesses and trusts with multiple contacts, authorized signers, portal users, responsible partner, assigned team, service scope, lifecycle and consent preferences. A contact may represent several entities without sharing their private records. Search, edit, archive and reactivate clients without losing engagement history. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a business with two contacts and an individual linked to its owner; assign different staff and portal permissions, change a contact, archive/reactivate the business and inspect retained jobs. A contact without entity authority cannot read its records.

- **CRM-002 — Client groups and custom fields.** Group related entities and households, capture typed firm-defined fields and tags, filter a segment and apply reviewed bulk updates or work creation. Required-field validation and permissions apply to imported and API-created records too. **Acceptance evidence:** Group a parent, subsidiary and household; add a fiscal-year and service-tier field, filter the group and schedule its annual work. Preview a bulk field change, reject one invalid value and confirm unrelated clients are unchanged.

- **CRM-003 — Engagement history and accountable ownership.** Each client and engagement has a searchable chronological history of email, portal messages, documents, requests, assignments, reviews, deadlines, signed scope and billing outcomes. Every unresolved item has a responsible person, next action and due condition; closing a message alone does not complete the engagement. **Acceptance evidence:** Open a client after two periods of work and trace a request from email through upload, review and payment. Filter unresolved items, change their owner and inspect authors, source links and timestamps; a preparer sees only authorized history.

- **ENGAGE-001 — Proposals engagement letters and signatures.** Build service proposals and versioned engagement letters with scope, exclusions, USD fees, retainer terms, renewal and amendment rules. Send to authorized signers, collect electronic signatures with signer authority, consent, timestamps and a downloadable certificate, then launch onboarding once. Preserve declined, expired, superseded and partially signed states; tax authorization signatures use the specialist identity/KBA process required by the form. **Acceptance evidence:** Send a two-signer proposal, amend it before completion and verify the old version cannot bind the new terms. Complete signatures, download the signed letter/certificate and inspect one onboarding job; decline another and exercise a specialist tax-signature handoff.

## Work deadlines and team delivery

- **WORK-001 — Reusable engagement templates.** A principal or reviewer creates reusable monthly, quarterly or annual templates containing a checklist, document request list and fixed fee. Starting an engagement copies the template into its own period work so staff have concrete tasks and requests. **Acceptance evidence:** Through the app or authenticated HTTP doors, create a recurring template, start a client job and inspect copied checklist, requests, recurrence, fee, preparer and reviewer. A preparer cannot create templates.

- **WORK-002 — Automatic recurring work and roll forward.** Approved recurrence rules create the next period automatically with assigned staff, fresh requests, dependencies, budget and calculated due dates. Support monthly closes, quarterly payroll/sales tax, annual returns and custom schedules, including fiscal-year ends. Preview the schedule, pause/skip a period and catch up after downtime without duplicate client-period engagements; historical sign-offs remain immutable. **Acceptance evidence:** Enable monthly and fiscal-year recurrence, let scheduled work become due and inspect exactly one next-period job with fresh requests. Restart the scheduler, pause a client, skip a period and override a due date; no duplicate or silently altered prior job appears.

- **WORK-003 — Dependencies and conditional workflow.** Templates define task dependencies, conditional branches, required evidence and preparer/reviewer gates across jobs. Completion events advance only eligible steps, with visible reasons for blocked work; a dependency cycle is rejected and an authorized exception requires a recorded reason. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a close feeding a tax engagement, branch on an organizer answer and complete prerequisite tasks. Inspect automatic advancement and blocked successors; reject a cycle and record an authorized exception without fabricating evidence.

- **WORK-004 — Bulk work scheduling and assignment.** Preview and create or reschedule work for a selected client segment in bulk, resolving preparer/reviewer roles, staff eligibility and client-specific exceptions. Changes show impacted jobs and conflicts before approval and preserve existing work and independent review. **Acceptance evidence:** Preview quarterly jobs for a client group, override two assignees, exclude one client and commit. Re-submit the same batch and inspect no duplicates, then bulk reassign work while refusing inactive or self-review assignments.

- **WORK-005 — Firm work queues.** Staff see a due-date-ordered work queue with client, period, preparer, reviewer, status and outstanding checklist/document counts. Preparers see their assigned jobs; principals and reviewers see firm work and can open its detail. **Acceptance evidence:** Create two jobs for different preparers, open the work queue as each role and inspect dates, assignees, status and outstanding counts. Directly requesting another preparer's hidden job is refused.

- **WORK-006 — Personal plans boards and calendars.** Provide a weekly personal action plan, Kanban boards and firm/client calendars filtered by service, assignee, due date, blocked status and priority. Moving a card respects workflow gates and changes the underlying job; calendar changes show deadline and capacity consequences. **Acceptance evidence:** Plan a preparer's week from the overdue queue, move an eligible board card and inspect the same job in calendar and client views. A blocked review transition is refused and filters persist after refresh.

- **DUE-001 — Sourced US obligations and extensions.** Provide maintained federal and firm-selected state deadline templates for 1040, 1120-S, 1065, 941 and sales tax, with entity/period/fiscal-year applicability, weekend/holiday rules, state/local frequency and jurisdiction-specific extensions or disaster relief. Import IRS and state revenue calendar updates with source URL, effective date and rule version. A firm approves applicable rules and material changes, sees recalculated due dates and can override with reason; original, extended and internal dates remain distinct. Extension work stays open until the tax authority supplies filing evidence. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure all five obligation types for clients in two states and different fiscal years. Calculate a weekend/holiday case, import a source revision and preview affected jobs before approval. Record an extension and override; verify separate original/extended/internal dates, preserved provenance and no claim of filing from a checklist.

- **DUE-002 — Tax notices and resolution work.** Capture a federal/state notice with its source file, agency, tax period, response date, assigned practitioner, authorization and resolution plan. Track research, client questions, agency contact, response submission and follow-up until an acknowledged resolution or documented exception; link transcripts and filing receipts without treating them as tax advice. **Acceptance evidence:** Open a notice from a client upload, verify the response deadline, assign a practitioner, link an authorized transcript and response receipt, and schedule follow-up. A failed response stays open; closing requires evidence and a recorded outcome.

## Client communication portal and documents

- **MAIL-001 — Gmail and Microsoft 365 shared inbox.** Connect Gmail/Google Workspace and Microsoft 365/Exchange mailboxes with scoped OAuth, shared/delegated mailbox access and visible sync health. Triage threads into clients and engagements, assign email as work, comment internally, send/reply with the authorized identity and file attachments against the correct job. Preserve message/thread identity, timestamps and authors, prevent duplicate sends/imports, and quarantine ambiguous client matches. Revocation stops access and surfaces unsynced work. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect a real authorized Google mailbox and Microsoft shared mailbox, receive and reply to client threads, delegate one as a job and file an attachment. Send the same event twice, introduce an ambiguous sender and revoke access; inspect one copy, explicit triage and actionable sync failure.

- **COLLAB-001 — Contextual discussions and review alerts.** Staff discuss a client, task, document or review in internal threads with mentions, ownership, unread state and retained decisions. Submission notifies the assigned reviewer; replies and requests for changes return to the preparer with a due follow-up. Internal material never leaks to portal users. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit work, receive the assigned reviewer's notification, mention a colleague and request a correction. Resolve it as the preparer, inspect the retained thread and verify that the client and unassigned staff cannot read private discussion.

- **PORTAL-001 — Private document requests and uploads.** Staff send outstanding document requests using a private expiring upload link. The client can upload files without an account; staff download, accept or return them with a precise note. Link rotation revokes its predecessor, job completion closes collection, raw link secrets are not retained and upload limits are explicit. **Acceptance evidence:** Send a request, rotate its link, refuse the old link and cross-origin upload, upload within the displayed limit and reject an oversized file. Download and accept the files, sign off the job and verify collection closes.

- **PORTAL-002 — Authenticated branded client portal.** Each firm has a branded portal and custom domain with client login, invitations, recovery, two-factor authentication and per-entity/contact access. Clients switch among explicitly authorized entities, manage notification preferences and view approved documents, signatures and bills. Firm policy can require MFA; revoked contacts lose existing-session access server-side. Scoped upload links remain available as a separately controlled collection method. **Acceptance evidence:** Invite two contacts with different entity access, sign in with a second factor on the firm domain and recover a lost factor. Revoke one contact mid-session and attempt direct document/API access; inspect refusal without exposing another entity.

- **PORTAL-003 — Client progress secure chat and to dos.** Clients see engagement milestones, next action, outstanding requests and review/completion progress, and exchange secure messages or to-dos attached to the right engagement. Staff control what is client-visible; a client response updates the outstanding item and alerts its owner without marking unrelated work complete. **Acceptance evidence:** Open an engagement as a client, ask a secure question, complete one to-do and inspect the preparer's queue and progress view after review. Confirm internal notes and another entity's tasks remain hidden.

- **PORTAL-004 — Conditional organizers.** Create versioned tax and bookkeeping organizers with conditional questions, required fields, prior-period carry-forward clearly marked for confirmation, supporting uploads and multi-contact responsibility. Clients save and resume, and staff review changed answers and completeness without losing the submitted original. **Acceptance evidence:** Send an organizer, answer an ownership question that reveals a dependent section, save/resume on a phone and attach evidence. Submit, correct one answer and inspect both versions and the resulting request list; unconfirmed carried answers stay unresolved.

- **MOBILE-001 — Mobile client and practitioner work.** Responsive web and native iOS/Android apps let clients scan/crop multi-page documents, resume interrupted uploads, answer organizers, chat, sign and pay; staff triage, reassign and review work with role-appropriate access. Push notifications use consent and reveal no tax details on a lock screen; offline drafts disclose sync state and revoked sessions cannot sync new work. Keyboard and assistive-technology access remain available on the web. **Acceptance evidence:** At desktop and phone widths and on iOS/Android, capture a multi-page statement, interrupt/retry the upload, complete a client task and review it as staff. Inspect push privacy, offline-to-online conflict handling, keyboard navigation and revocation.

- **DOC-001 — Client files versions and annotations.** Store engagement documents in client/period folders with searchable names, source, author, timestamps, version relationships, annotations and review status. Preserve originals and prior versions; downloads and annotations respect permissions. Support normal multi-page accounting packets with published limits, resumable uploads and clear failure recovery instead of silently truncating files. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload a realistic multi-page packet, replace it with a corrected version, annotate a page and find both versions from the client and engagement. Interrupt an upload and resume; compare downloaded originals and inspect limit/error handling.

- **DOC-002 — Document sharing and internal only custody.** Per-document visibility supports internal-only workpapers, selected client contacts and revocable/expiring approved shares. Preview recipients and access before sharing, audit downloads and preserve visibility on versions, connector copies and migration. External sharing never bypasses client or job permissions. **Acceptance evidence:** Keep a workpaper internal, share a final packet with one contact, rotate/revoke its share and attempt direct reads as another contact and preparer. Inspect inherited version permissions and retained access history.

- **FOLLOWUP-001 — Automatic document chasing and escalation.** A firm-approved reminder sequence follows missing documents, organizer answers, signatures and unresolved questions through email, portal and opted-in US SMS via Twilio. Configure owner, business hours, cadence, maximum sends, pause and manager escalation. Re-evaluate outstanding items before each send, stop completed/declined items and record provider acceptance, delivery or failure separately. Respect SMS consent and STOP/HELP, firm sender registration and provider outages; an unanswered notification never resolves the blocker. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve a bounded reminder sequence, leave two items missing and complete one between sends. Observe only the remaining item chased, a failed delivery surfaced to its owner and escalation at the configured condition. Reply STOP to an actual consented US SMS and verify further SMS ceases; pause and resume without duplicate sends.

## Review ledger context and capacity

- **REVIEW-001 — Independent reviewer sign off.** The preparer completes all checklist and accepted-document gates, submits to the assigned reviewer and receives approval or a recorded request for change. Sign-off requires a note, rejects preparer self-review, records who and when, closes collection and prevents later checklist edits to signed-off evidence. **Acceptance evidence:** Attempt premature submission, complete the evidence and submit. Refuse preparer sign-off, approve as the assigned reviewer with a note, inspect identity/time and verify the closed portal and blocked signed-off checklist edits.

- **LEDGER-001 — QuickBooks and Xero client ledger context.** Connect QuickBooks Online/QuickBooks Online Accountant and Xero organizations to the correct clients, showing read-only transaction, balance, reconciliation and period-close context with source links, last successful sync and explicit stale/partial state. Reconnect and multi-client permission changes preserve mappings. Reading client books grants no permission to write ledger entries or access the firm's billing ledger. **Acceptance evidence:** Authorize QBO Accountant and Xero client organizations, map them to two engagements and inspect a transaction, balance and close context against each source. Revoke one grant and change the period; stale context is labelled and never silently treated as current.

- **LEDGER-002 — Transaction questions and close evidence.** Ask clients precise questions against source-ledger transactions and period reconciliations, attach their answers/documents and carry unresolved issues into preparer/reviewer work. Compare current and prior periods, flag unusual movements and retain the source snapshot and staff disposition. A reply alone cannot book an adjustment or certify a close. **Acceptance evidence:** Select an uncategorized transaction, request its business purpose, receive an answer and receipt, then return it for more evidence. Inspect the source-linked thread in review, a prior-period variance and the recorded reviewer decision without any unapproved ledger write.

- **CAPACITY-001 — Workload capacity and coverage.** Plan staff hours by week, skill, service, availability, leave and non-client commitments against estimated remaining work and review demand. Show overload, unscheduled work and deadline conflicts across the firm with drill-down to jobs; distinguish estimates from actual time. **Acceptance evidence:** Load a tax-season week with preparer/reviewer budgets, scheduled leave and a client meeting. Inspect available hours, overloaded staff and affected jobs; adjust an estimate and confirm totals and conflict explanations update.

- **CAPACITY-002 — Reassignment and dependency aware scheduling.** Reassign and reschedule work when staff are absent or overloaded, proposing eligible coverage with client authority, skills, remaining capacity, dependency order and independent reviewer constraints. Preview notifications and affected deadlines; a manager approves the plan and owns unresolved conflicts. **Acceptance evidence:** Remove an assigned preparer for leave, preview alternate staffing and move a dependent job chain. Approve the feasible changes, refuse an ineligible/self-review assignment and inspect new owners, notifications and unresolved deadline conflicts.

- **CAPACITY-003 — Capacity forecasts and hiring plans.** Forecast future workload from approved recurring engagements, historical completion/time, pipeline and seasonality, with visible assumptions and confidence ranges. Compare staffing, hiring or service-change scenarios without altering actual assignments or promised deadlines. **Acceptance evidence:** Project the next tax season from recurring work and prior actuals, add a hypothetical hire and lost client, and compare demand/capacity by skill. Trace estimates to sources and save a scenario without changing live work.

## Time billing payments and reporting

- **TIME-001 — Time budgets and activity suggestions.** Record time against client/job/task with manual entry and timers, billable status and snapshotted rate; compare actual hours/cost with approved budgets. Suggest missing entries from consented work/email/calendar activity with source links, never silently creating billable time. Corrections retain authorship and respect locks. **Acceptance evidence:** Record time, change the rate and inspect unchanged prior amounts and budget variance. Stop/restart a timer, reject one activity suggestion and approve another; verify only approved minutes enter WIP and unauthorized edits are refused.

- **TIME-002 — Timesheet approval and period locks.** Staff submit timesheets to managers, who approve or return entries with reasons. Closing a billing period locks approved time; corrections use an authorized adjustment that preserves the original. Invoice/export reservations prevent the same work being billed twice. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit a weekly timesheet, return and correct one entry, approve it and close the period. Attempt direct edits and duplicate billing, then post an authorized adjustment and inspect its history.

- **BILL-001 — Reviewed immutable billing exports.** Reviewers prepare time/fixed-fee CSV drafts with snapshotted sources. The principal approves once with a note; the approved bytes and digest remain unchanged and each source belongs to one live export. Draft voiding releases reservations; CSV cells neutralize spreadsheet formulas and preserve source currency. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare a mixed time/fixed-fee export, reject source reuse and non-owner approval, approve as principal and download twice. Verify identical CSV bytes, source locks, formula-safe cells and a voided draft's released sources.

- **BILL-002 — Invoices retainers and recurring fees.** Create numbered USD invoices from approved time, fixed scope, milestones or recurring retainers; separate fee entitlement from job completion. Preview invoice lines, terms, authorized sales-tax treatment, credits and client recipients, then approve and deliver PDF/portal invoices. Retainer deposits, credits, price changes, cancellation and prorations remain traceable. Select exactly one invoice authority: Blotter, QuickBooks Online or Xero; schedule generation is idempotent. **Acceptance evidence:** Bill a fixed engagement and hourly extra, start a monthly retainer, apply a deposit and preview a fee amendment. Approve/deliver invoices, repeat the schedule event and inspect one invoice per entitlement with USD totals and retained source links.

- **PAY-001 — Card ACH and authorized collections.** Collect invoices by card and US ACH through Stripe, with saved-method mandates and explicit authorization for recurring or due-date automatic collection. Show fees in USD, payment terms and ACH pending/settled/returned states; distinguish provider acceptance from settlement. Verify webhook signatures, deduplicate events, reconcile partial payments, refunds, disputes and returns, and own failed-payment follow-up. Provider onboarding, US bank access and payment-method migration from TaxDome Payments are engineering obligations; nonportable tokens require client reauthorization. **Acceptance evidence:** Pay a real authorized low-value invoice by card and ACH, authorize a recurring mandate and inspect provider receipts and ledger reconciliation. At the provider test edge exercise duplicate/out-of-order events, ACH return, partial payment, refund and failed collection; revoke the mandate and verify no later debit.

- **BILL-003 — WIP receivables realization and profitability.** Report unbilled WIP, earned/deferred retainers, aged receivables, collection follow-up, credits, write-ups/downs, billed-versus-recorded realization, utilization and client/job/service profitability using approved time, cost and invoice/payment sources. Drill into calculation inputs, exclude duplicate exports and keep uncollected, settled and written-off amounts distinct. Partners approve credits, write-offs and collection messages. **Acceptance evidence:** Run WIP, aging and profitability for a client with hourly work, fixed fees, a retainer, partial payment and credit. Reconcile totals to approved sources and provider state, approve a write-off and inspect the changed period report without rewriting historical invoices.

- **REPORT-001 — Firm performance and client reporting.** Principals report work status, overdue/blocked jobs, turnaround, client response delay, review rework, deadlines, staff utilization, capacity and profitability by client/service/team/period. Saved views, scheduled internal reports and permission-checked CSV/PDF exports link to source records and state data freshness. Client-facing reports contain only approved client-visible data. **Acceptance evidence:** Complete and delay several engagements, filter a period/team and drill from turnaround and overdue totals to source jobs. Schedule a partner report, export it and compare totals; a preparer/client cannot obtain the firm's private financial report.

## Connections and extensibility

- **INTEGRATE-001 — Firm accounting invoice and payment exchange.** Connect the firm's QuickBooks Online or Xero billing organization separately from each client's ledger grant. Map customers, services, tax codes, invoice IDs, credits and payments to the chosen invoice authority; preview direction and conflicts before enabling writes. Replay an event, change a closed-period invoice externally and inspect one source of truth and a visible conflict.

- **INTEGRATE-002 — US tax software document and status handoff.** Support Lacerte, ProSeries, Drake, UltraTax CS and CCH Axcess through authorized connectors and desktop print/upload bridges where needed. Map client/entity/year, deliver source documents and retrieve final packets, signature requests and filing/status receipts available from the tax authority. Preview unsupported fields and require practitioner confirmation for ambiguous matches; no handoff implies a return was calculated or filed. **Acceptance evidence:** For each named tax stack, send an approved client-year packet through its supported connector/desktop path, receive a final packet and filing-status evidence and inspect IDs/provenance. Disconnect the bridge and verify blocked work remains owned and no false filed state appears.

- **INTEGRATE-003 — Connected document storage.** Connect Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint and Dropbox with explicit folder/client mapping, least-scope authority and clear original/copy/link custody. - **INTEGRATE-005 — Calendars and client appointment booking.** Connect Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar for staff commitments and job deadlines with configurable direction, time-zone/DST handling and private-event masking. Inspect capacity and the client timeline, masked private events and removal of cancelled commitments.

- **INTEGRATE-006 — Authorized IRS transcripts.** Retrieve IRS e-Services/TDS transcripts through the authorized practitioner account and specialist integration with required taxpayer authorization, identity verification and permitted data use. Track request, pending, received, unavailable and failed states, source period and freshness; reconcile transcript differences into notice/review work. Never bypass practitioner credentials, authority restrictions or agency access rules. **Acceptance evidence:** Using an authorized practitioner/taxpayer pair, request a permitted transcript, receive it into the correct client-period and link a discrepancy to review. Exercise absent authorization, expired practitioner access and pending/failure responses without a fabricated transcript.

- **INTEGRATE-007 — Gusto payroll engagement context.** Connect Gusto payroll clients with permission to read payroll work items, run status and due-date evidence, route unresolved payroll requests into practice work and link the authoritative payroll record. A completed practice checklist cannot mark payroll paid, taxes remitted or a 941 filed without source evidence. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect an authorized Gusto client, observe a payroll work item and missing-information exception, follow its run status and inspect the linked source result. Revoke access and verify stale status and owned follow-up without payroll money movement.

- **API-001 — Authenticated product HTTP doors.** Authenticated HTTP doors expose native clients, templates, work, checklist, time, billing exports and role-appropriate context. Direct route calls obey the same actor, job visibility and human-approval rules as the app; agent keys cannot invoke person-only doors. **Acceptance evidence:** Create/read work and time through the authenticated HTTP API, inspect billing as reviewer and refuse preparer/private-job reads, signed-out access and agent-key use on person-only routes.

- **API-002 — Documented API webhooks and Zapier.** Publish a versioned client/work/time/document/billing API with scoped revocable integration keys, pagination, filters, idempotent writes and explicit rate limits. Signed webhooks and Zapier triggers/actions support owner-configured cross-app workflows with field mapping, delivery history, replay control and conflict handling. External credentials never inherit broader owner authority. **Acceptance evidence:** Connect Zapier to create a client and task from an approved event, read paginated work/time and receive a signed completion webhook. Replay a write/event, revoke a key and inspect deduplication, bounded scopes and actionable delivery failure.

- **AUTOMATE-001 — Durable firm automation.** Firm-reviewed schedules and events coordinate recurrence, conditional step advancement, email filing, review notifications, reminders, onboarding and due-date billing. Each rule has an owner, client scope, budget, preview, pause/stop, durable attempt/outcome and visible next action. Recovery deduplicates accepted effects, rechecks authority and reconciles ambiguous provider outcomes before repeating external messages or charges. **Acceptance evidence:** Enable rules through the app, trigger file/task/proposal and due-time events, interrupt processing after provider acceptance and restart. Inspect one authorized effect, retained attempts, a visible ambiguous/failing item and no work after rule pause or authority revocation.
