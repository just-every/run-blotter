# First release

A principal of a 1–15 person Australian bookkeeping or accounting practice can:

1. **Team and roles.** Add reviewers and preparers, set their hourly rates and
   invite them; preparers see only the jobs assigned to them, and deactivation
   removes access at once.
2. **Roll a job forward.** Start a client's quarterly BAS job from a template
   (checklist, document requests, fixed fee), then roll it forward to the next
   period with a due date a person records.
3. **Collect documents.** Send the client a reminder with a private upload
   link; the client uploads the requested files without an account, and staff
   accept them or ask again.
4. **Review and sign off.** The preparer submits the finished job; the assigned
   reviewer (not the preparer) signs it off with a recorded note.
5. **Export time for billing.** Time is recorded at the person's snapshotted
   AUD rate; a reviewer prepares an export of time and fixed fees, the
   principal approves it once, and the CSV never changes afterwards.
6. **Agent preparations.** An agent key prepares roll-forwards, reminders and
   billing exports; a person approves or declines each, and the decision is
   recorded.
7. **Move in.** Preview, import, inspect the journal of and undo a client list
   CSV (Base import-kit); undo keeps clients that already have jobs.
8. **Safety.** Download the whole-practice archive and restore it into an empty
   installation; erase a client file's bytes so older archives are refused
   without the newer erasure journal.

Not needed for the first release: lodgement (SBR or the ATO), tax or GST
calculation, due-date calculation, client bookkeeping, client e-signature,
advice, invoice delivery or payments, a client login, real provider calls in
tests, brand or homepage design, a run site or domain purchase. Files are
limited to 1 MiB each.
