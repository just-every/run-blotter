# Blotter change history

## 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-27 — Product scope and proof provenance

This is the product destination. Status below describes current code, not a reduction of that destination. The previous first-release ACCEPTANCE.md described an Australian BAS-style workflow with manual deadlines, link uploads, independent review and billing CSVs; there was no root CONTRACT.md or existing product clause ID to rename. Its useful guarantees are retained here. The Status table classifies each clause as published, target or push-further, including implemented foundations. Existing UI, homepage and runtime behavior are unchanged by this document.

Every named clause gives buyer behavior and an acceptance journey through the app, authenticated HTTP API, CLI or authorized provider door. ACCEPTANCE.md lists the same journeys and statuses. A cited local file/test proves only its stated implemented subset; it does not prove real-provider acceptance, tax authority access, native distribution, production capacity or accessibility. Real-provider journeys use authorized accounts; automated tests fake only paid/network edges. Source freshness, provider acknowledgment, settlement and human judgment are distinct facts.

Current statuses describe this candidate tree. The exact commit and its verification runs are reported in the branch handoff; a status claim is counted only after the guarded landing and CT judgment. Due dates are recorded by people, set by WORK-002 rules or calculated from approved deadline rules (DUE-001, DUE-003).

**Current implementation (COST-001 remains partial; AI-003):** The principal records sourced USD quotes before enabling native plans. Prices are estimates, not verified provider tariffs. Included units are shared lifetime-to-date under one price revision; usage retains that revision and its quote. Percentage estimates round up once per action in integer USD cents. Optional native plans reserve cost atomically with one preparation; a blocked event is retained with a principal-owned in-app exception and can be retried under the same action id after an approved service-budget increase. A per-action threshold and lifetime ceiling both apply; reconciliation never replays an effect or releases the conservative reservation. Changing a quote disables it until reviewed again. Unbuilt paid providers stay unavailable. Explicit upgrade and replacement quotes, JSON usage export and native firm archives provide the exit path.

The native deterministic planner grants one principal-owned live key, one active client, job metadata and outstanding-request labels and one preparation tool (reminder or roll-forward). It never invokes a model or supplies document bytes, private or internal discussions, vault data or financial context. A schedule uses an inclusive offset-bearing start instant and an hourly slot of its configured interval; skipped intervals are not replayed. Stable file, task and question events produce at most one plan per rule, event and job. Plan budget exhaustion is retained as a refusal; service-budget exhaustion remains retryable with the same event id. Both plan commit and human effect batches recheck the key, active principal, client, source and rule. A key bound to these rules cannot bypass their budgets through legacy preparation and learning doors; its context is restricted to active client grants. Revocation does not fabricate a human decision. Archives retain content-free run and cost references and decisions, exclude keys, and restore rules paused and prices disabled; fresh native keys and explicit review are needed for a new grant. Client erasure revokes grants even under a hold and redacts eligible derived rule text through ERASURE-002. A keyed external caller may claim only its queued event metadata through GET `/api/blotter/agent/plans` and fill it once through POST `/api/blotter/agent/plans/:id`; its provider, model, data-use and retention declaration is visible and explicitly unverified. Blotter makes no provider request. Further model integrations and grants, data and tools in AI-001/AI-002 and READY-001/RESCUE-001/REVENUE-001/ONBOARD-001/SWITCH-001/NOTICE-001 inherit these authority, budget, attribution and restoration duties before shipping. ct approved the principal-only metadata and preparation-tool grants with human-only effects on 23 October 2026 at 10:15Z. The key value is returned only at creation; later reads and the operator UI never redisplay it. Provider, model, data-use and retention declarations remain explicitly unverified in the operator grant, external plan and human decision view. COST-001 retains its separate provider-reconciliation Gap.

Blotter owns practice CRM, engagement scope, workflow, inbox, portal, document/request custody, review, capacity, time/WIP, invoices, collections and follow-up. It remains its own product. General firm file storage connects to [Chest](https://github.com/just-every/run-chest), Google Drive, OneDrive/SharePoint or Dropbox under INTEGRATE-003; client workpapers and request evidence stay part of Blotter. It does not absorb an unowned neighboring category.

Tax-return calculation, tax advice and federal/state e-filing stay with the qualified practitioner and Lacerte, ProSeries, Drake, UltraTax CS or CCH Axcess. DUE-001 owns calculated obligation deadlines and extension workflow, INTEGRATE-002 owns the packet/status bridge, and INTEGRATE-006 owns authorized IRS transcripts. Completed work is never evidence of a filed or accepted return.

QuickBooks Online and Xero remain authoritative books. LEDGER-001/002 bring client close context into practice work; INTEGRATE-001 separately connects firm revenue accounting. Gusto remains the payroll calculation, payroll payment and remittance authority; Blotter owns the related practice work and status evidence, not a second payroll engine. Payroll: [Envelope](https://github.com/just-every/run-envelope).

Stripe supplies card/ACH processing, regulated onboarding and bank settlement. Blotter owns invoices, mandates, payment-state reconciliation and exceptions under PAY-001. Karbon Payments, TaxDome Payments, Canopy Payments and Financial Cents payment history is migrated, and saved methods move to valid mandates or become tracked client reauthorizations under MIGRATE-007. Signature/KBA providers supply specialist identity verification; Blotter owns the engagement-letter journey and signed evidence. Required commercial/provider agreements and US sender registration are implementation clauses, never exclusions.

Google and Microsoft supply email/calendar transport; Twilio supplies US SMS transport; booking providers supply appointment booking. Blotter retains consent, authority, delivery distinctions and owned failure follow-up. AI reads only authorized practice data under visible provider terms, can be paused, and never replaces professional judgment or creates source evidence. USD fees and US buyers come first; additional jurisdictions require explicit sourced templates and acceptance.
